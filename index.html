FAQ

Questions, answered.

Quick answers about privacy, AI tools, formats and the desktop app.

Is APIC really free?

Yes. APIC is a free online photo editor that runs in your browser. There are no paid tiers. You can edit with every tool without an account, and you sign in with one tap using Google only when you export your work.

Are my photos uploaded to a server?

No. Editing, converting, compressing, resizing, background removal and object removal all run on your own device. Your images never leave your browser. Signing in only uses your name, email and photo for your account.

How does the AI background remover work?

APIC runs an AI segmentation model directly in your browser using WebAssembly. The first time you use it, the model downloads once (about 5 MB for Lite, 44 MB for Fast, or 176 MB for Maximum) and is cached on your device, so later runs start much faster and nothing is sent to a server.

Which image formats can I convert between?

APIC converts between PNG, JPEG, WebP, BMP, ICO and PDF. Use PNG or WebP when you need to keep a transparent background after removing it.

Can I compress an image to a specific file size?

Yes. The Compress tool searches for the best quality that fits your target file size, so you can hit an upload limit without guessing quality settings.

What can the Remove Object tool do?

Paint over something you want gone, such as a person, sign or blemish, and an on-device AI inpainting model fills the area to match its surroundings. Larger or more complex areas work best with the Ultra engine on desktop-class devices.

Why is the first AI run slower?

AI tools need to download their model the first time. After that it is cached in your browser, so repeat use is fast. If the download fails, APIC retries automatically and shows a Retry button if it still can't connect.

Is there a version that works offline?

Yes. APIC for PC is a Windows app that runs fully offline with the same edit, convert and compress tools. You can download it from the section below with no account needed.

Does APIC have presets for social media profile pictures and banners?

Yes. Open the Social tab to pick an exact-size preset for a profile picture, banner or cover, post or story on Instagram, YouTube, Facebook, X, TikTok, LinkedIn, Pinterest, Snapchat, Threads, Bluesky, Discord, Twitch, Spotify and more. Drag and zoom to frame your photo, check the safe-zone guides to see where each platform crops or covers your image, then download one size or a ZIP of every size for a network. Everything runs on your device, and APIC keeps files under each platform’s size limit automatically.

Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 28, 2026

APIC is a product of Akhouri Systems, developed by Akhouri Anmol Kumar. This page explains, in plain words, what APIC does with your data.

Your images stay on your device

Editing, converting, compressing, resizing, background removal and AI tools run inside your browser. Your images are not uploaded to our servers. The first time you use an AI tool, your browser downloads a model file from a third-party host (see below), which is a normal file download.

What we collect when you sign in

  • Through Google Sign-In (handled by Firebase Authentication) we receive your name, email address and profile photo. We never see your Google password.
  • We store those details in Firebase Firestore, along with your first and last sign-in time and how many times you signed in.
  • We keep anonymous totals such as page visits and member counts. These contain no personal information.

How we use it

  • To run your sign-in and show community numbers.

To keep APIC free, one small banner ad from Adsterra is shown in the page footer. It is loaded in its own frame, separate from the editor, and APIC never sends your photos to it. Adsterra may receive technical data such as your IP address, browser and device type, and may use cookies or similar identifiers to choose and measure ads, under its own privacy policy. As with any ad network, we rely on Adsterra to follow its own privacy policy. If you prefer no ads, the offline APIC for PC app shows none.

Third-party services

APIC relies on Google (Sign-In, Firebase, Firestore, Fonts, model hosting), EmailJS, jsDelivr, cdnjs, Hugging Face, GitHub (hosting and downloads), and Adsterra (the header banner ad). These services may see technical data such as your IP address when your browser contacts them, and each follows its own privacy policy.

We keep your account record until you ask us to delete it. To see, correct or delete your data, or to stop emails, use the Contact tab. You can also sign out any time.
