Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 28, 2026

APIC is a product of Akhouri Systems, developed by Akhouri Anmol Kumar. This page explains, in plain words, what APIC does with your data.

Your images stay on your device

Editing, converting, compressing, resizing, background removal and AI tools run inside your browser. Your images are not uploaded to our servers. The first time you use an AI tool, your browser downloads a model file from a third-party host (see below), which is a normal file download.

What we collect when you sign in

  • Through Google Sign-In (handled by Firebase Authentication) we receive your name, email address and profile photo. We never see your Google password.
  • We store those details in Firebase Firestore, along with your first and last sign-in time and how many times you signed in.
  • We keep anonymous totals such as page visits and member counts. These contain no personal information.

How we use it

  • To run your sign-in and show community numbers.
  • To send you a welcome email and occasional APIC update emails (sent through EmailJS). You can ask us to stop at any time.
  • We do not sell your data, and APIC does not show ads.

Third-party services

APIC relies on Google (Sign-In, Firebase, Firestore, Fonts, model hosting), EmailJS, jsDelivr, cdnjs, Hugging Face, and GitHub (hosting and downloads). These services may see technical data such as your IP address when your browser contacts them, and each follows its own privacy policy.

Keeping and deleting data

We keep your account record until you ask us to delete it. To see, correct or delete your data, or to stop emails, use the Contact tab. You can also sign out any time.

Children

APIC is not intended for children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their data. If you think a child has signed up, tell us and we will remove the account.

Security and changes

We use reputable services and reasonable safeguards, but no system is perfectly secure. If this policy changes, the date above will change too.

Cookies & Storage

Last updated: September 28, 2026

APIC does not use advertising or tracking cookies. It uses your browser's local storage for a few small things:

  • apic_user: keeps you signed in on this device (your name, email and photo). Removed when you sign out.
  • apic_watermark_demo_used: remembers that you already used the free demo.
  • apic_notice_ok: remembers that you closed the notice about this.
  • Google and Firebase may store their own identifiers so sign-in works securely.
  • Your browser may cache the AI model files so they do not download again.

You can clear all of this any time in your browser settings. Doing so will sign you out.
